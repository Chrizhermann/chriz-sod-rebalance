# v0.6.13 — early BG1 companions and an interim Avernus finale

Released October 13, 2026. It combines three approved,
separately developed changes on top of v0.6.12. The installer now exposes
**46 declarations in seven groups**.

## Player behavior

- **198 — early BG1 companions.** Skie can join in the palace hall before the
  first rest. Yeslick, Branwen and Ajantis stand outside the palace; Coran and
  Garrick at the Elfsong; Kagain, Alora and Xan in the Elfsong district; Tiax in
  his Flaming Fist cell; Kivan and Shar-Teel at the Coast Way camp. Conversations
  are short: join, decline, rejoin later. A companion you brought from BG1 keeps
  their build and belongings; known deaths are respected and never replaced.
  Fresh starts use modest BG1-level versions with normal SoD join XP. Dismissed
  recruits follow the army to the Coast Way, Troll Claw and Coalition camps.
  The palace bedroom uses the normal Rest button for preparing spells.
  Faldorn, Quayle, Eldoth, Xzar and Montaron remain for a later round.
- **177 — report Korlasz to Liia later.** If you fight Korlasz after the first
  night, Liia's ordinary and departure-day conversations accept the report for the
  same once-only 22,000 XP per present party member. The journal points you to her
  until you report. Already-paid rewards stay paid.
- **285 — Avernus portal and a fairer final battle.** The lower tower switch is a
  one-way portal straight into the existing confrontation; the lift ride and its
  battles are skipped without replacement XP. Belhifet needs +1 weapons, has 60%
  magic resistance and 25% extra movement, with 350/400/450/475/500 HP on
  Easy/Normal/Core/Hard/Insane. Delayed targeted Breach replaces the opening mass
  dispel. Devil reinforcements are capped (two on Core, four on Hard and Insane)
  and fill one vacancy at a time. Hephernaan keeps his devil form with a finite
  14/14 cleric/mage spellbook. Confrontation and victory are unchanged.
- **170/900 — loot identification.** Relocated Korlasz jailbreak loot and Coast
  Way camp-chest treasure keep the original item's identification state instead
  of arriving identified. Reward contents, counts and charges are unchanged.

## Install timing and limits

- 198 requires 110 and 197; 177 requires 175 and 180 (after 176 if present).
  Both are intended for a new SoD start; behavior when added to a save already
  inside SoD is not verified.
- Install 285 before first entering BD4700, preferably from a save made before
  the lower tower (BD4600). Saved area or creature data keeps its old setup.
- Existing saves that already contain the relocated loot are not changed.
- No new native playtest is claimed. Recruitment, camp follow-up, Liia's late
  report, bedroom resting, the Avernus portal and both boss fights still need the
  short in-game checks below.

## Short in-game check

1. New SoD start: Skie joins before the first rest; recruit one city companion
   and one Elfsong companion; dismiss one and find them at the Coast Way camp.
2. Fight Korlasz after the first night, then report to Liia once; check the XP
   and that a second report is not offered.
3. Check one relocated jailbreak or camp-chest item starts unidentified.
4. Use the Avernus portal, then fight Belhifet and Hephernaan on Core (and
   Insane if convenient): devil count stays within its cap, Breach is delayed,
   the victory sequence plays normally.
