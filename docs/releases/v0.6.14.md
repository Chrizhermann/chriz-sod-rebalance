# v0.6.14 — Imoen's handover into Shadows of Amn

Released October 18, 2026. It adds one EET-only choice on top of v0.6.13. The
installer now exposes **49 declarations in seven groups**.

## Player behavior

- **292 / 293 / 294 — Imoen in Shadows of Amn (EET, choose one).** Before the
  campaign switch, an imported or recruited Imoen's SoD gear goes through EET's
  import rules like the party's: items on EET's import lists are imported (one
  copy, only when the party has none), and the rest goes to EET's hidden import
  bank. She keeps only her BG2 belt, now correctly equipped. This fixes the
  off-party Imoen who reached SoA with all her gear and her belt pushed into the
  magic-weapon slot, so she fought bare-handed.
  - **292** (CEBG default): she becomes EET's Shadows of Amn Imoen (`IMOEN10`)
    plus her BG1/SoD tome boosts, each score capped at 25. No EEex needed.
  - **293**: she continues as taken along, with SoA's dialog, scripts and XP
    catch-up.
  - **294** (requires EEex): a pure single-class thief dual-classes to mage at
    her end-of-SoD thief level. SoA's catch-up XP goes into mage, and she learns
    `IMOEN10`'s wizard spells. Kit, HP, skills and proficiencies stay.

## Install timing and limits

- EET only. Requires 160, 290 and EET_end; 294 also requires EEex. Works with
  910 in either install order.
- Install before leaving SoD. Installing into a game already in Shadows of Amn
  is not tested.
- **Known issue (open):** if Imoen is dead when SoD ends, SoA's opening scene
  never gives control back, because EET has no case for a dead Imoen who has
  been in the party. This already happens with 160 and 290 alone; raise her
  before the ending.

## Verification boundary

- Lab on a disposable copy of the CEBG install (EET, EEex, Spell Revisions,
  engine 2.7.3.0); details in the
  [playtest note](../playtest/2026-10-17-imoen-soa-handover.md):
  - 292 and 293 passed on both routes (290's ending and 910's bedroom skip) for
    an imported Imoen in the party, one dismissed elsewhere or in the bedroom,
    and a recruited `csrimo`.
  - 294 passed on the ending route with and without a kit and for a recruited
    `csrimo`. EET's own `IMOEN10` stays unchanged, and a reload does not convert
    her again.
- Not covered: a game without Spell Revisions (synthetic installer tests only);
  thief kits other than `C0TRICKS`; 294's new mage levels through the level-up
  screen; a BG1 Imoen dismissed in BG1 together with 910's skip and an
  unrecruited `csrimo`.
- CI (Ubuntu, WeiDU 249) on the release source: 388 regression tests pass (28
  skipped where a captured fixture or the development game copy is not
  available), as do the 41 research-script tests and the ending self-test's 14
  checks. WeiDU 249 parses the TP2 and all 73 TPA libraries.
