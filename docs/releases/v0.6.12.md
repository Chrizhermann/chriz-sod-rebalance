# v0.6.12 — Boareskyr Bridge collapse timer

Release dated September 27, 2026. Fresh component **256** now includes the
approved timer; append **258** to an older 256 without uninstalling it.
The installer exposes **43 declarations in seven groups**. Optional **257**
still adds only the two Insane mage sequencers.

## Player behavior

| Difficulty | Unpaused allowance |
| --- | --- |
| Story Mode | No timer |
| Easy | 10 minutes |
| Normal | 8 minutes |
| Core Rules | 7 minutes |
| Hard | 6 minutes |
| Insane / Legacy of Bhaal | 5 minutes |

The clock starts with the encounter's opening warning. At 25%, 50% and 75%
elapsed, the protagonist receives overhead warnings, also recorded in the log,
with native rockfall sounds and brief increasing shakes. Defeating both mages
and all four elementals immediately confirms that the bridge is safe; guards
and other crusaders cannot keep demolition running. Bence's combat-clear
aftermath stays separate. A last-second victory takes priority over expiry.

Failure gives a short collapse message, rocks, a black fade and the native
game-over screen. It does not reuse the barrel explosion or kill the party.
Save/reload preserves the deadline and warnings. Ordinary difficulty changes
do not reset the budget. Enabling Story Mode permanently disables this
encounter's deadline. Retreat preserves it, with completion/expiry checked
when the party returns to the bridge.

## Upgrade and compatibility

- Prefer a pre-fight save. An older ongoing stage-2 encounter gets a fresh
  budget on its first update after 258; an already-completed encounter is unchanged.
- On an existing 256, 258 changes only the area controller and adds its small
  failure helper plus text. Actor AI, stats, spellbooks and bridge artwork remain
  as installed. On fresh 256, 258 makes no resource or dialogue-table changes.
- Khalid/Skie companion patch ordering retains the existing guarded routes.
- Removal and petrification retain the encounter's existing defeat semantics,
  so Spell Revisions Banishment can stop demolition. Native Maze suspension is
  indistinguishable from removal to these triggers; temporarily removing the
  final threat can also resolve demolition. This inherited limitation remains.
