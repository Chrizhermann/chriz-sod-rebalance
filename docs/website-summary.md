# SoD Remix — website copy

Editorial draft for a dedicated SoD Remix subsection on bg.chrizfader.org.
Checked on 4 October 2026. The public copy begins below; version, component and
verification details are in [the separate source note](website-summary-sources.md).

## Introduction

SoD Remix reshapes Siege of Dragonspear into a shorter, more focused adventure:
less repetitive combat, fewer interruptions, more freedom to keep your party,
and selected encounters rebuilt to make the fighting more interesting. It
reworks the opening, trims the journey north and gives the campaign a cleaner
ending, while retaining the major siege battles. Its changes are modular, with
optional challenges and choices about removed-content rewards.

## What the current release changes

### A quicker opening and a clearer story

- Skip the opening Korlasz dungeon. Begin with the aftermath of your victory
  over Sarevok, a palace celebration and Caelar's proclamation; face Korlasz and
  her allies in a rebuilt jailbreak encounter instead.
- Remove the palace assassination and Imoen-poisoning sequence, along with its
  leftover dialogue references. Entar remains dead, preserving BG1 continuity.
- Remove the Hooded Man's appearances and the chapter dreams. The dwarven
  ruins' scrying puzzle stays, but its visions become one brief omen about Caelar.

### Keep the party you want

- Carry your BG1 companions into SoD instead of having the game forcibly
  scatter them. Fresh SoD starts can also avoid the automatically assigned party.
- Recruit Imoen at the palace and take Skie along as a straightforward companion,
  with normal companion XP catch-up and without Skie's original SoD subplot.
- Recruit Rasaad at the Coast Way camp after his original map is removed.
  Khalid's Bridgefort scenes adapt when he is already in your party, keeping
  him under your control while Adirran handles the local briefing.

### Less attrition along the road

- Rest interruptions are substantially less frequent: the reduction targets
  the chance of being interrupted across a full rest.
- Remove Coast Way Forest and bypass the Forest of Wyrms bugbear cave, linking
  the dragon cave directly to the temple.
- Replace much of the dwarven dig site's undead crowding with a smaller set
  of placed encounters. Trim repetitive groups around Coast Way Crossing,
  Troll Claw Woods, Boareskyr, the Forest of Wyrms, Dead Man's Pass, Bloodbark
  Grove, the Underground River and Kanaglym.
- Keep the large battles that belong to the siege. This is a selective cut of
  filler, not a blanket removal of armies or every travel encounter.

### More purposeful fights, fewer frustrating restrictions

- Korlasz's jailbreak becomes a prepared enemy-party encounter with support
  roles and a difficulty-dependent roster.
- Boareskyr Bridge replaces the explosive-barrel finale with two mages, two
  veterans and four fire/earth elementals. Stop the demolition before the bridge
  collapses, with warnings and a deadline that scales by difficulty. Story Mode
  has no deadline; defeating the mages and elementals stops the clock.
- Morentherene gains stronger combat statistics on Hard and Insane, giving the
  dragon more staying power without adding a new spell routine.
- Keep the assassin ambush while removing its dead-magic restriction. Remove
  the forced party dispel at the Dragonspear basement reveal and the invisible
  barrier at the early Coast Way bridge fight, which also gives you longer
  before the parley starts.
- Remove specified Unsleeping Guardians and Shadowed Soul encounters or summons,
  and prevent Shadow Aspect from repeatedly casting Mislead on Insane.

### Rewards and quest continuity

- Move compensation XP into retained quest and chapter milestones so cutting
  repetitive fights does not simply erase their contribution to progression.
- Preserve affected quests and important items, including Ymori's quest and an
  obtainable source for Mizhena's amulet.
- Choose whether notable treasure from the removed Coast Way Forest is collected
  in the camp chest or removed with that content.

### A shorter ending, plus optional extras

- Keep the playable victory celebration, then finish SoD without Skie's murder,
  arrest, trial, prison escape and the subsequent epilogue chain. EET continues
  through its normal BG2 opening and equipment-import rules; standalone SoD
  ends at the credits.
- An optional **Extra Challenge** strengthens the two bridge mages on Insane
  with spell sequencers. The regular bridge overhaul already includes its
  other encounter improvements and collapse timer.
- A separate experimental **EET-only full skip** offers a confirmed choice at
  palace arrival to go straight from the BG1 continuation into BG2, with normal
  carried-equipment handling and a one-time protagonist XP award.

## Upcoming work — separate from the current release

- **Avernus and Belhifet: implemented locally, awaiting playtesting and release.**
  A one-way portal replaces the elevator ride and its battles. The interim finale
  lowers the weapon and magic-resistance barriers, replaces the opening mass
  dispel with delayed targeted Breach, reduces devil pressure and gives Belhifet
  more staying power and movement. Hephernaan gains a prepared caster role,
  with a lighter setup below Insane. This keeps the existing final encounter
  while the larger ending rewrite is developed.
- **Ashatiel: encounter design in progress.** A group-versus-group brawl is planned
  as a third choice alongside the original solo duel and refusal. The direction
  includes a bounded arena and time to prepare; the roster, individual builds
  and difficulty tuning still need decisions.
- **Later campaign work.** The long-term direction makes Caelar the final boss
  and revisits the Avernus story. Further encounter and filler revisions,
  clearer XP-award presentation and better placement/identification of relocated
  loot remain on the backlog. These are directions for future work, not announced
  release contents or dates.
